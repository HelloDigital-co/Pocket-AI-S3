Feature states & UI views

Clock & Daily Briefing (v1)

Shows live local weather, moon phase, step count, and the AI Daily Briefing card synced from your localhost.

Local MP3 / WAV Player (v1)

Plays local audio tracks stored on the MicroSD card over onboard speaker or Bluetooth A2DP.

Hold-to-Record & Bookmarks (v1)

Elapsed recording timer, bookmark counter, live level meter, and touch/button capture directly to MicroSD.

AI Agent Local Q&A & Transcript (v1)

Conversational Q&A grounded in your notes, with brain source indicator, Hold-to-Talk, New Chat, and Transcript viewer.

Recordings & Sync Tabs (v1)

Four-tab browser (RECS, NOTES, TASKS, SYNCED) showing recorded clips, durations, and sync status.

Playback with Synced Transcript (v1)

Audio scrubber and pause/+15s controls paired with timestamped Whisper transcript lines—tap any line to seek.

Full Recording Transcript View (v1)

Speaker-labeled Whisper transcript with highlighted active timestamp line and mini playback scrubber.

Tasks List & Status (v1)

Displays open and AI-suggested tasks with due dates, assignees, and source meeting links.

Task Detail & Audio Jump (v1)

Shows full task text, due date, source meeting, one-tap audio jump (▶ 3:11), and Mark Done.

Tasks AI Chat & Breakdown (v1)

Ask your AI Agent follow-up questions on any extracted task, verify transcript context, read aloud, or mark done.

Focus Timer & Alarm (v1)

Circular Pomodoro countdown ring with 5m/10m/25m/45m presets and daily alarm indicator.

Precision Stopwatch & Laps (v1)

Centisecond stopwatch with live lap splits and delta timing.

Battery & System Summary (v1)

AXP2101 voltage, runtime estimate, display brightness, Wi-Fi RSSI, and SD free space.

Squatchy RF & BLE Scanner (v1)

Passive BLE + Wi-Fi + Dex tracker scanner with Radar, List, Wi-Fi, and Dex tabs plus live device detections.

Squatchy Companion (v1)

Step-goal & detection-driven pocket pet whose health blends today's steps/goal (60%) and detected BLE/Wi-Fi devices (40%).

MeshCore LoRa Peers (v1)

Off-grid 915 MHz peer signal strength, hop count, ping, and voice note actions.

PIN Lock & Emergency Rec (v1)

4-digit touch keypad protecting device data while keeping emergency record one tap away.
